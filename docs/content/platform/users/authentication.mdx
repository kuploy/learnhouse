# Authentication

LearnHouse supports multiple authentication methods to fit different deployment scenarios.

## Login Methods

- **Email and password** — the default authentication method for every LearnHouse instance.
- **Google OAuth** — sign in with a Google account.
- **Kuploy account** — when your instance is run from a Kuploy site, people can sign in with their account there.

For enterprise deployments, SAML (via WorkOS) and Custom OIDC are available — see [Enterprise SSO](/enterprise/sso).

## Security

Logins are rate-limited per IP, and accounts are temporarily locked after repeated failed attempts. SaaS-mode deployments additionally require users to verify their email address before they can sign in.

## Signup Mechanisms

Organizations can control how new users join the platform:

- **Open signup** — Anyone can create an account and join the organization.
- **Invite-only** — Only users who receive an invitation can create an account.

The signup mechanism is configured per-organization in the [organization settings](/platform/organizations/settings).

If your instance offers a sign-in with the account of the Kuploy site that runs it, a new person who arrives that way joins an open-signup organization automatically, when the organization has room. On an instance with several organizations, it is the one they signed in from, when the browser passes it along. Signing in again does not add someone through open signup. Invite-only organizations still require an invitation.

<Callout>
Choose invite-only signup for private or internal deployments where you want to control exactly who has access.
</Callout>

## Session Management

Once authenticated, users maintain a session via JWT tokens. The backend issues an access token (8-hour expiry) and a refresh token (30-day expiry) that keep users signed in across page loads.
